01 ABOUT ME

Programming has the power to add behavior to artworks to express contemporary ideas about everyday life.

I’m a university teacher and a visual artist. I was born in Rio Gallegos, Santa Cruz, but I grew up in Buenos Aires.

My job is to guide my students in their personal projects and teach them how to design, communicate and develop their ideas, especially in digital arts like programming, video games, AI, art installations, animation, robotics, etc.

My personal work is about VR for mobile devices and also make artistic dolls because I love modeling things with my hands as well as programming.

I enjoy working with light too. For example in this video you can watch a robotic doll with light and music.
